常见问题

拍照后没有识别出文字？
请确保页面光线充足、文字清晰对焦。倾斜角度过大时建议正对书页重拍。

AI 讲解次数用完了？
免费版每天可用 3 次完整的 AI 讲解，次日自动重置；LingoLens Pro 不限次数。

如何管理或取消订阅？
iOS 设置 → 你的 Apple 账户 → 订阅 → LingoLens，可随时更改或取消。 也可在 App 内「设置 → LingoLens Pro → 恢复购买」找回已有订阅。

我的照片和学习记录会上传吗？
不会。照片、文字识别和全部学习记录只存在你的设备上。 详见隐私政策。

FAQ

Text not recognized after photographing?
Make sure the page is well lit and in focus. If the angle is steep, retake the photo facing the page directly.

Out of AI explanations?
The free tier includes 3 full AI explanations per day, resetting daily. LingoLens Pro removes the limit.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
iOS Settings → your Apple account → Subscriptions → LingoLens. You can also restore an existing subscription in-app via Settings → LingoLens Pro → Restore Purchases.

Are my photos or learning records uploaded?
No. Photos, text recognition, and all learning data stay on your device. See our privacy policy.
